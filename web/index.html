About the readings

NASA POWER estimates incoming sunlight on a horizontal surface. All-sky energy includes clouds. The clear-sky benchmark estimates the energy that would reach that surface under cloud-free conditions.

Each point represents a grid area around the city. Rooftop yield also depends on panel angle, efficiency, shading and snow. The 2001–2020 comparison averages the 20 values for each calendar month, giving each year equal weight.
